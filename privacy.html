Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

BizzView shows a business's Tally data on the phones of the people it chooses, through the BizzView app, the BizzView Connector on the Tally computer and our server. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and how to delete it. Questions: support@bizzview.in.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address, phone number (if given) and password (stored only as a secure hash). Accounts are created by BizzView or by your organisation's owner or admin.
  • Your phone: its model and platform, and an identifier the app creates, so your organisation can see which phone a login is used on and keep one phone per login.
  • Business data from Tally: the companies you choose to sync, with their ledgers (party names, addresses, GSTIN, phone numbers, email addresses), vouchers, bills and stock. The Connector reads this from Tally on your computer and sends it to our server. It never changes your existing Tally data; entries you create in the app are sent to Tally only when you submit them.
  • Location and photos, only when you use them: where you are when you check in or out of a party visit, a photo you attach to a visit, your signature or company stamp for invoices, and price lists you upload. The app asks for permission first, and uses location only while you are checking in or out.
  • The assistant, if your organisation turns it on: the questions you type or speak and the answers. Voice recordings are converted to text and are not kept.

We do not show ads, we do not sell or rent data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Why we use it

  • To show you your organisation's Tally data and reports, and to create the entries, parties and items you ask for in Tally.
  • To sign you in, keep your account secure and let your admin control who sees what.
  • To produce the invoices, statements and reminders you share.
  • To answer your questions in the assistant, when it is on.

Who processes it for us

  • Amazon Web Services hosts our server, database and files in Mumbai, India.
  • Expo delivers app updates to your phone; it does not receive your business data.
  • AI providers (Anthropic or OpenAI) answer assistant questions, and speech-to-text providers (OpenAI or Sarvam AI) turn voice questions into text, only for organisations that use the assistant. They receive the question and the business figures needed to answer it, and may not use them to train their models.
  • Our GST service provider (a GST Suvidha Provider) looks up GSTINs and prepares e-invoices and e-way bills, only for organisations that use these features.

We share data with authorities only when Indian law requires it.

Who can see it

Data belongs to the organisation that synced it. Inside an organisation, the owner and admins decide which companies, screens and parties each person sees. BizzView staff access an organisation's data only to provide support or when the law requires it.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as hashes. Photos and files are kept in private storage. The Connector uses its own token, and each login can be used on one phone at a time.

Keeping and deleting data

  • We keep data while your organisation uses BizzView.
  • Delete your account in the app: More › Delete my account. A staff account is deleted at once: your name, email, phone and password are erased and you lose access to every company. Entries you made stay in your organisation's books.
  • The owner's account carries the whole organisation, so deleting it deletes the organisation, its users and all data synced from Tally. We complete this within 7 days of the request in the app, or of an email to support@bizzview.in.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Nothing is deleted from Tally: your books on your own computer stay as they are.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to support@bizzview.in. We answer within 30 days.

Children

BizzView is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above and, for important changes, tell owners in the app or by email.
