Set up in three steps

You install a small connector on the computer that runs Tally. It keeps your phone up to date, so you never export or email a file.

  1. 1

    Install the connector

    Download it on the Windows PC where Tally runs, then sign in with your email.

  2. 2

    Choose your companies

    Open your companies in Tally and add the ones you want to see in BizView.

  3. 3

    Install the app

    Get the BizView app on your Android phone and sign in with the same account. Your data appears.

Questions

Is my data safe?

Data is encrypted while it syncs and is only available on devices you sign in with. The admin decides who sees what.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Your data is stored on the phone after it syncs, so you can read reports offline. Entries sync when you are back online.

How many companies can I add?

As many as your Tally licence holds. Open them in Tally, then add them in the connector.

Can my salesperson see everything?

No. You choose which customer groups each person can see. Profit and loss, bank balances and expenses stay hidden.

What does the Windows app do?

It is the connector. Install it on the PC where Tally runs, and it keeps your phone in sync with your Tally companies. You only set it up once.
